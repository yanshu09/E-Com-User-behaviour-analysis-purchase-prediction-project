Business Interpretation

  • Prioritize high-intent sessions: predicted high-intent sessions show substantially higher observed conversion in the test set.
  • Focus on engagement signals: PageValues is the dominant predictive feature in the Decision Tree, followed by BounceRates and several engagement/context features.
  • Use segmentation carefully: the model predicts session purchase likelihood; it does not establish customer-level lifetime value or causality.
  • Next production step: connect session-level intent scoring with customer IDs, purchase history and campaign outcomes before deploying personalized interventions.
